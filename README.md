Description:

*A story about day to day troubles of life...* *Or is it...*

CAN YOU MAINTAIN ALL YOUR RELATIONSHIPS? CAN YOU BECOME WHAT YOU DESIRE OR WILL YOU FALL TO CHAOS AND CONFUSION?

Inverted Skies is a game about expectations, interpretation and how your smallest choices can cause ripples across reality.
The story follows Ash, a foreign student in japan, trying to figure out his life like most teens with existential crises Yet it all falls apart as he realises that reality and people aren't as they seem at first glance, that even the most concrete views on life can be turned upside down within seconsds.
He must earn the trust of his fellow students, and partners, to find out what truly is behind the chaos while staying alive.

\\*How to play:*

Playing the game is as simple as can be.
Load up the game page on itch.io through this link: https://ashvn.itch.io/inverted-skies
And the game should load automatically, but even if it doesn't, just hit play/load and if that option doesn't appear refresh the page and try again.
Once you are at the menu, hit play and the game should work, if anything breaks hit the rollback button and save, and retry. 

If the game doesn't load that maybe due to low ram, or a lack of device storage, this is especially the case for mobile devices.
Since this game is optimized for Pc and web devices.

\\*Why I made the game:*

This is inverted Skies, a game that takes inspiration from Everlasting summer by Soviet games, steinsgate, and a few more.

I wanted to make a game that reflected the confusion and chaos of day to day life, even if the writing isn't great it's my attempt of conveying the regrets I had not choosing to do the things I desired and reflect that you can change the world for the worse or for the better even if you aren't someone of great status, wealth, looks or popularity.

I also wanted it be as introspective as Everlasting Summer by soviet games, while incorporating the mysteries of steinsgate.
Another game which inspires me Is Null. That game, surprisingly had a huge impact on my life even though I didn't get the story much.
